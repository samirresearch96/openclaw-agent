Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

OpenClaw Agents is a personal, single-user application. It accesses only the Google account of its owner, with the owner's explicit consent.

Data accessed

Using Google OAuth, the app may access the owner's Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Tasks, Contacts and related data, solely to carry out tasks the owner requests.

How data is used and stored

Data is processed on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are stored locally in an encrypted keyring. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Where the owner asks an AI assistant to work with their data, content is sent only to the AI provider the owner has chosen, for that request only.

Retention and revocation

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete local data at any time.
